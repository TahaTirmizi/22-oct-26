Find out roughly where you stand on GRE Quant today, and about how many hours of prep it will take to reach your target score.

What to expect

  • 24 questions covering the four GRE Quant content areas: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, and Data Analysis.
  • All four GRE question formats: Quantitative Comparison, single-answer multiple choice, select-all-that-apply, and numeric entry.
  • 42 minutes, the same pace as the real test (about 1 min 45 s per question).
  • You can skip questions, flag them, and come back to them before you submit, just like on the real test.
  • Keep scratch paper and a basic calculator handy. The real GRE gives you an on-screen calculator.

Question review

The score estimate weights hard questions more heavily than easy ones and maps the result onto the 130–170 scale. The prep hours come from a rule of thumb: each point costs more as you get closer to 170. Both are approximate. Retake after a few weeks of study, and take official POWERPREP tests to check your progress.
